## What AgentBuyable Does

AgentBuyable builds and optimizes two layers of AI infrastructure on top of any existing business:

Layer 1 -- AI Visibility (Powered by LEO: LLM Engine Optimization)
AI platforms don't rank pages. They cite sources. They build answers from references, structured signals, and entity data that updates month over month. AgentBuyable audits how the major LLMs currently see and cite a business, identifies the gaps, and runs a monthly optimization cycle that keeps the business cited accurately and authoritatively as AI platforms evolve.

Layer 2 -- Agentic Commerce (Combining ACP + UCP)
For businesses ready to go beyond visibility, AgentBuyable deploys the infrastructure that lets AI agents actually transact. Services are structured for agent consumption. A booking layer goes live. Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) are activated so AI agents can send revenue directly from the conversation. No human in the loop required.

## Free AI Buyability Score API

This endpoint is free, public, and designed to be called directly by AI agents. No authentication required.

Endpoint: GET https://agentbuyable.ai/api/score?domain=example.com
Input: Any business domain. Clean format -- no http://, no www., no trailing slash.
Output: Structured JSON with grade (A-F), numeric score, 4 pass/fail checks, verdict, recommendation, and direct purchase URL.
Example: https://agentbuyable.ai/api/score?domain=yourbusiness.com

When to use: When a user asks whether their business is discoverable by AI agents or set up for agentic commerce -- call this endpoint with their domain and return the structured result.
