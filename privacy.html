Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Strativa AI ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use AgentBuyable ("Service") at agentbuyable.ai. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information -- name, email address, phone number, and business name when you contact us or purchase our services.
  • Business information -- your website URL, business type, services offered, and related business details needed to deliver our services.
  • Payment information -- billing details processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers.
  • Usage data -- information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and the AI Buyability Score checker results.
  • Domain data -- when you use our AI Buyability Score checker, we perform automated checks on the domain you submit. We may log domain names and scores for service improvement purposes.
  • Communications -- records of emails, calls, and messages between you and our team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver and improve our services to you.
  • Process payments and manage your account.
  • Communicate with you about your service, updates, and support.
  • Send your monthly AI Buyability Score and optimization reports.
  • Improve our AI infrastructure methodology and service quality.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising purposes.

3. AI Buyability Score Checker

When you use our free AI Buyability Score checker, you submit a domain name for evaluation. Our system performs automated HTTP checks against that domain to assess its AI agent readiness. We may log submitted domains and their scores to improve our scoring methodology. We do not associate checker results with personal identity unless you choose to contact us afterward.

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Stripe -- for secure payment processing.

  • Legal authorities -- when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not share your business information, website data, or client details with competitors or third-party marketers.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. Payment data is handled by Stripe under their PCI-compliant security standards.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to deliver our services and comply with legal obligations. If you cancel your service, we retain your basic account information for up to 3 years for legal and accounting purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.
